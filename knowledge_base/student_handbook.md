# Fee Payments
Students can view their fee balance and due date through the student portal. For a failed payment, save the transaction reference and contact the Accounts Office. A payment extension is not automatic and must be confirmed by the Accounts Office.

# Scholarship Information
Scholarship decisions depend on the scheme and institutional review. Students should submit the required application and documents by the published deadline. Personal scholarship status must be retrieved from the student's own record.

# Course and Elective Changes
Students should submit a written request to the Programme Office during the add/drop period. Approval depends on seat availability and academic rules. A change is final only after written confirmation.

# Student Portal Support
For portal access issues, contact the IT Helpdesk or submit a support ticket. Never include passwords or OTPs in a support request.

# Attendance
Students should consult the official Programme Office for the current attendance requirement and consequences. This sample handbook does not specify a numeric minimum attendance percentage.
